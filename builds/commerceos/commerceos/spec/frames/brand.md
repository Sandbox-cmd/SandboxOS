# frame — the brand discovery (F1, the name)

state: frame draft — 2026-07-18. ruled 2026-07-18: research, ideate,
propose — areas, themes, names, naming styles, best practices, branding,
design, sound, tone, voice — and the owner decides from the outcome and
moodboarding. the name is NOT picked in a sitting; it resolves at the
moodboard stage (B3).

## the two ruled constraints

1. **grounded**: outdoor and adventure retail, UAE/GCC, bilingual —
   Arabic as real as English (C2 is a customer promise, not a checkbox).
2. **umbrella-survivable**: the name must survive category expansion
   beyond outdoor — more projects are coming; the store is product #1.

every candidate direction is tested against both, in writing.

## the axes the divergence explores (B2 picks 5–8 directions across these)

- **construction**: descriptive (says what it sells) ↔ evocative (says what
  it feels like) ↔ coined (a made word) ↔ place/heritage-rooted.
- **language**: English ↔ Arabic-rooted ↔ bilingual play (a word that reads
  in both) ↔ transliteration-neutral (sounds the same in both scripts).
- **register**: expedition/heritage ↔ modern/technical ↔ warm/communal.
- **breadth**: category name ↔ umbrella name (the ruled constraint pushes
  umbrella; the test is whether it still sells a tent honestly).

## the tests every shortlisted name must pass

- **Arabic behavior**: transliterates cleanly, carries no awkward meaning in
  Gulf dialects, sits well in Arabic script on a storefront.
- **umbrella test**: put a non-outdoor product line under it — does it hold?
- **sound**: said aloud once, spelled right — phone-test (someone hears it,
  can they type it?).
- **practical rails**: .ae/.com domain realism, social handles, no obvious
  trademark collision in the GCC (checked at shortlist, not at ideation).

## what the research (B1) must answer before ideation

- how successful GCC bilingual retail brands actually handle the two
  scripts (one name transliterated vs two names vs English-only).
- naming-style patterns in outdoor/adventure retail globally, and what the
  regional incumbents sound like — where the whitespace is.
- sound-and-tone practice: what registers signal trust vs hype in the
  region; voice implications for the store's copy (ties to the content
  agent's voice guide).

## deliverables and the decision path

- `reports/brand/research-2026-07.md` — B1, cited findings.
- `reports/brand/directions.md` — B2, 5–8 directions, each with example
  names, Arabic behavior, umbrella test result, and a one-line why.
- `reports/brand/moodboards/` — B3, one board per surviving direction:
  look, type, tone, sample store lines in both languages.
- the owner narrows at B2, decides at B3. the decision is F1; store rails
  F2–F6 open behind it.

## what this lane is not

not a part spec, not code, and no code item may depend on a name existing.
the store keeps building true under its working name until F1 lands.
