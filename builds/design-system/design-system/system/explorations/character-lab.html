How it works & why it wins: a stylized bust is drawn with canvas form + gradients, converted to luminance, then error-diffusion dithered to 1-bit at high resolution — the same technique behind the dithered portraits on your board. Because it's code, every parameter is a knob: crop, expression, hair, shades, the accent hue, even animation (she can blink, the dither can shimmer, she can dissolve into the fabric and re-form). This is the unlock — she's a generative system, not an asset we have to source. (Higher-fidelity passes & true anime linework can layer on top; this is the engine.)

Three rungs, left→right: the old chunky grid, a finer ordered (8×8 Bayer) weave at 2px, and a blue-noise stipple at 1px that reads as soft analog grain rather than pixels. We can dial cell-size, algorithm, and contrast per surface — loud and chunky for slaps, silky and fine for ambient fabric. Move your cursor over each to lift it.

A cartridge is one build. A pack is a labelled set (a shelf, a season, a starter kit). Same physical language — supercolor spine, gold pins, serial — but color-coded by type (build / skill / persona). You collect them, trade them, and a 3D shelf holds your library; eject one to install.

Provenance built ineach cart: serial, edition (001/500), © NOBODY, a fork-lineage you can trace.
The shelf is the home screenyour installed packs as a rotating 3D rack — the OS's main navigation is physical.
Eject = uninstallreal gestures: slot to install, eject to remove, snap two carts to combine builds.
