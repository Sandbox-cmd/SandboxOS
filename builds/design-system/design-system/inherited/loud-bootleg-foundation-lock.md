# Loud Bootleg OS as foundation premise (owner lock, design-system project)

At GATE 2 of the design-system deep dive, the agent synthesis recommended surface-tiering
(loud-where-earned, legible-where-decided), consolidating onto GHOSTLINE, and promoting the
"Living Gradient Schematic" to a tier-A trust base. Hameed overrode all three on taste/vision:

- Bootleg OS is the base AND the dominant register (not GHOSTLINE — he dislikes it).
- Playful/loud is the FOUNDATION premise expressed at every level, not a tier.
- The "Living" Schematic is out (he finds it cringe); Bootleg dominates.
- Stake a new category; full production pass; license variations to be explored, he picks.

Note: not a scar — the report's tiering analysis was sound; the owner chose a different vision he owns.

overrides analysis and that override is the amana. Register-neutral blockers (broken form a11y,
missing dense primitives, Google-CDN fonts, civilian-legible public copy) survive "loud everywhere"
and are NOT softened by it. Full record: studio/2026-06-20-ds-deepdive/frame-lock.md +
