## Note on evidential weight

The major signs are, by the tradition's own account, unfulfilled future events. An unfulfilled prediction is not evidence for anything: it cannot be checked, so it carries zero weight at step 4 or anywhere else on the chart. This folder exists only to catalogue the signs for completeness and to answer the common skeptical move of mixing major (unfulfilled) and minor (claimed-fulfilled) signs together. No argument file should cite a major sign as evidence.

The canonical list of ten comes from the hadith of Hudhayfah ibn Usayd in Sahih Muslim (commonly cited as Muslim 2901 — verify): the Smoke, the Dajjal, the Beast, the sun rising from its west, the descent of Isa ibn Maryam, Ya'juj and Ma'juj, three landslides (east, west, Arabia), and the fire from Yemen that drives people to their gathering place. The Mahdi is not in this list and rests on different sources.

## The signs

- **The Dajjal (the False Messiah)** — a deceiving figure performing counterfeit wonders, the greatest fitnah before the Hour. Extensively attested: Sahih al-Bukhari and Sahih Muslim in many narrations (e.g. the long hadith of al-Nawwas ibn Sam'an, Muslim 2937 — verify), plus the hadith of Tamim al-Dari (Muslim).
- **The descent of Isa ibn Maryam** — Jesus returns, kills the Dajjal, breaks the cross, and rules justly. Bukhari and Muslim (e.g. Bukhari 2222 "the son of Maryam will descend among you as a just ruler" — verify number); also read into Quran 4:159 and 43:61 by many exegetes (interpretation, not explicit text).
- **Ya'juj and Ma'juj (Gog and Magog)** — destructive hordes released near the end, overwhelming the earth until destroyed by divine intervention. Quran 18:94-99 and 21:96-97; hadith in Muslim (within the al-Nawwas ibn Sam'an narration) and Bukhari (the opening of the barrier, e.g. Bukhari 3346 — verify).
- **The Beast (Dabbat al-Ard)** — a creature brought forth from the earth that addresses/marks mankind. Quran 27:82; listed among the ten in Muslim 2901 (verify); details beyond this are from weaker or exegetical material.
- **The sun rising from the west** — after which repentance is no longer accepted. Bukhari and Muslim (e.g. Bukhari 4635/6506 — verify); linked by exegetes to Quran 6:158 ("some of your Lord's signs").
- **The Mahdi** — per Sunni sources, a man of the Prophet's household named Muhammad ibn Abdillah who fills the earth with justice; he is not named in Bukhari or Muslim as "al-Mahdi" (Muslim's "a caliph who pours out wealth" narration is read as him by some — verify). Main texts: Sunan Abi Dawud (the Kitab al-Mahdi, often cited as 4282-4290), Jami' al-Tirmidhi, Sunan Ibn Majah. Gradings vary by narration from sahih to very weak; a minority (famously Ibn Khaldun) questioned much of the corpus. Any future file must sort narrations individually.

Related major events usually catalogued with these (same caveat applies): the Smoke (Quran 44:10-11; Muslim 2901), the three landslides, the fire from Yemen (both in Muslim 2901 — verify).
